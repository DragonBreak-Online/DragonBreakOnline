# DragonBreak Online Staff Handbook

**Version 2026-10-21. In force from 21 October 2026.**

For every staff rank: Trial GM, GM, Lead GM, Developer and Owner. It replaces the Trial GM Handbook (13 October 2026) and the GM Handbook (15 October 2026), which were kept as Claude documents, and takes the ticket rules from the Staff Rota draft (30 September 2026). The Staff Code of Conduct (21 September 2026) and the Staff Standard Operating Procedure (2 October 2026) are part of it.

Everything here comes from those texts, from rulings by Jake or Nate, or from what the game enforces.

The Server Rules apply to staff exactly as to every player. This handbook adds what is expected of you as staff.

---

## 1. The ranks

Your rank comes from your Discord role. After a promotion, log in again to get your new tools. If your role is lowered while you are in the game, you are logged out so the change takes effect.

| Rank | Adds, on top of the rank below |
|---|---|
| **Trial GM** | GM calls, Admin chat, teleport to a player or a named place, Invisible and Ghost, rename, `/unstuck` for others, read `/bug` reports, look up a character, character names instead of "Stranger" (`/realnames`), character creation for your own character, one staff character slot |
| **GM** | The full staff panel to look things up, Summon, Kick, God and NoClip, character creation for anyone, warnings and strikes, approving executions, announcements, the hub reset (`/fixloc`) |
| **Lead GM** | Items, spells, skills, beast forms and curses; placing NPCs and objects; appointing and dismissing officials; jail; suspensions; disciplining staff; citizenship and elections as staff; striking votes; referral decisions; faction leader, remove and override; time and weather; warbands and raids; restraining anyone at once |
| **Developer** | Bans, deleting a character from the staff panel, wiping every character of an account (`/wipechars`, see 5.2), and scheduling restarts (`/schedule`) |
| **Owner** | Everything |

**Final decisions** are made by Jake or Nate, who have equal authority. Where this handbook says "Jake or Nate", either one decides.

## 2. Rules for every rank

### 2.1 The Staff Code of Conduct

1. **Staff are not above the rules.** You follow every Server Rule, as staff and as a player.
2. **Staff powers are never used for an IC advantage.** What you learn as staff is OOC information.
3. **No positions of power.** Staff may not hold positions of power or leadership in factions, and may not make faction decisions, in public or behind the scenes. Ordinary membership is fine. Section 2.2 is the exact list.
4. **Stay neutral when you run roleplay.**
5. **Keep staff knowledge apart from character knowledge.**
6. **Say when you have a conflict of interest.** Do not handle a case you are involved in when another staff member is available.
7. **Do not use your rank to win OOC disputes.** When you are playing, you are a player.
8. **Every staff action needs a real purpose.** Never as a joke, revenge, a favour or a convenience.
9. **Keep staff matters confidential.**
10. **Do not protect friends or faction members**, and do not punish anyone harder over a personal or IC rivalry.
11. **Write major GM decisions down** in the staff channels.
12. **Do not undo roleplay without good reason.**
13. **Calm OOC conflict down.**
14. **Enforce the rules the same way for everyone.**
15. **Abusing staff powers is a serious violation.** It may lead to removal from staff as well as normal discipline.
16. **Staff characters are players too.** Your rank gives a character no extra IC authority, protection, knowledge or importance. The game still gives the characters of GM rank and above two protections, which exist for staff testing: a player must ask before restraining them (unless that player is a Lead GM), and the Thalmor cannot arrest them under the Concordat. Never use either to get out of a scene or an arrest: play it as any player would.
17. **The story belongs to the players.**

### 2.2 What staff may not hold

These limits apply to **every staff rank, Trial GMs included**, and to **every character on your account**, online or offline. The game reads your Discord roles from each character as it was when you last played it, so a character you have not played since you joined staff is not yet stopped by the game. The rule applies to it all the same.

| Role | Staff may hold it? | Does the game stop it? |
|---|---|---|
| Jarl, Count, Chieftain | No | Yes: "Staff hold no leading office in a court" |
| Steward, Lord Commander (Hold Commander), Guard Captain | No | Yes |
| Bane, Stronghold Guard Commander | No | Yes |
| Any seat on an election ballot (Jarl, Steward, Lord Commander, Housecarl) | No | Yes: "staff take no seat in a court" |
| A leader or officer rank in any faction, guild, college, cult or chartered faction | No | Yes: "Staff hold no leader or officer rank in a faction" |
| In a hold's household: Jarl, Steward, Guard Captain, Thane. In Bruma's: Count, Steward, Guard Captain, Knight. In a stronghold's: Chief, Bane, Stronghold Guard Commander, Hunt-Master | No (these are leader or officer ranks) | Yes |
| Housecarl (household rank) | No (Jake, 18 October) | Only on an election ballot. Outside an election the game does not stop it yet, so it is up to you |
| Noble titles: Baron or Baroness, Lord or Lady, Knight or Dame, Thane as an honour, Harbormaster | No | Not yet, so it is up to you |
| Guard, Stronghold Guard | Yes | |
| Court Mage, Shaman, Wise-Woman | Yes | |
| Sergeant, Mage, Blacksmith, Tailor or member rank in a faction | Yes | |
| Ordinary member of any faction, citizen of a hold | Yes | |

Office titles and faction rank names can be renamed at each court and in each faction. The game checks the office or the rank's role, not its name, and so should you.

Beyond the list: as staff you do not influence how a hold or a faction is run. Do not advise rulers or leaders, and do not steer their decisions. An idea for a ruler goes from a GM to a Lead GM, and then out, never from you to them in the game. (Jake, 18 October: staff count as the Aedra in character.)

### 2.3 Voting

Staff do not vote in hold elections. The game does not stop it, so it is up to you.

### 2.4 Your accounts and characters

- **One account.** You use one Discord account, like every player (Server Rules section 3). Never a separate staff account. An account for testing only with the Owners' approval.
- **Your staff character.** Every staff rank has one extra character slot, for staff work only: answering calls, checking reports, standing by during events. With it, do not trade, fight, earn, own property or join factions. Never give its items or gold to any player character, yours or anyone's. The game does not enforce this, so it is up to you.
- **Your other characters** play as players, with no staff advantages: no items, gold, information or protection from your rank. The limits in 2.2 and 2.3 still apply to them. An arrest made by any character of a staff member earns no bounty share (the game enforces this).
- **Race and sex changes for your own characters** need Jake's or Nate's okay, like anyone else's. Rerolls that come from a staff role are not a supporter's own reroll.
- **Your staff modes** work on whichever character you are playing. Turn them off before you play, and never use them for yourself or your friends.
- **Never handle a ticket, a call or an execution** that involves your own characters or your friends. Hand it to another staff member.
- **What you gather as a player** you may use or give like any player, through ordinary play, never through staff tools or your staff character.

### 2.5 Before you take a staff role

Step down from every role in 2.2 that staff may not hold, before the role is given to you.

### 2.6 Keep it private

- The staff panel shows Discord names and account details, and `/realnames` shows the name of a character other players see only as "Stranger". Never share them, or staff chat, logs or other players' details, outside staff channels.
- Never tell anyone how an election vote is going. Nobody sees the count until the vote ends, staff included.
- What you see as an Administrator in a faction's own Discord is OOC knowledge. Never use it in character or pass it on.

### 2.7 Every action is logged

Every staff command, every panel action and every refused attempt is logged with your name and rank. Act as if someone will read it, because someone will.

### 2.8 Never, on any rank below Developer

- Restart the server or change server files.
- Give items or gold to your own character.
- Promise a release date, a refund, compensation or a rule change.

### 2.9 Lore, and when in doubt

Lore follows Elder Scrolls canon. Do not invent lore for players: ask a Lead GM. When in doubt, ask before acting. A short wait is better than an action that cannot be undone.

## 3. One Discord account per person: how staff handle it

The Server Rules (section 3) allow one Discord account per person. The Privacy Policy limits how staff may look into it.

**Where the leads appear.** The PC check posts its lines (ALT CHECK, BANNED ACCOUNT BACK, NEW ACCOUNT, DEVICE CHURN, NOT AN ALT) in the staff audit channel. The dashboard shows them on the player's page. Lead GMs, Developers and Owners read these leads, decide on them and mark "Not an alt". A Trial GM or GM who sees a lead passes it to a Lead GM and does not act on it. The dashboard's **Not an alt** button needs its "manage players" permission: until Lead GMs have it, a Lead GM asks a Developer or an Owner to press it.

**You may use:**

- reports and evidence from players and staff (tickets, clips, screenshots, chat logs);
- what the player tells you when you ask;
- what Discord itself shows you;
- the game's own records, to look into a specific report;
- an "ALT CHECK" lead from the PC check when the other account is **banned**: it may point to ban evasion. A lead is never proof on its own: someone else in the same home may use that PC. Check it before you act. When it is not the same person, it is marked "Not an alt" on the staff dashboard.

**You may not:**

- use the PC check, or the "new Discord account" flag, to look for second accounts of players who are not banned;
- act against the other accounts an ALT CHECK says also play on that PC, when they are not banned: the check is about the banned account only;
- compare connection (IP) addresses to look for second accounts;
- ban or punish anyone because they share a PC or an internet connection with someone else. Two people in one home may each have their own account;
- tell any player another player's PC code, address, or why a check held their vote or referral.

**When you find a second account:** pass it to a Lead GM with the evidence. The Lead GM decides. The extra account may lose access: it is removed from our Discord and its game account closed through the account-deletion steps, with no ban entry, so it is not a ban of the person. The player chooses which account to keep, unless one is banned. The extra account's characters may be moved into the free character slots of the kept account, as in a move (below), unless the extra account was used to get around a ban or to gain votes or referral rewards: then they are closed with it. Any warning or strike goes on the kept account's record with `/discipline` (section 5.4); the closure is noted in the ticket. If a ban entry is used anyway, mark the kept account "Not an alt" for that ban, or the PC check will post the kept account as an alt of the closed one. If the extra account was used while a ban or suspension was running, it is ban evasion: pass it to a Developer or an Owner.

**Players who come forward** with a second account by 4 November 2026 are not punished, and nobody is punished just for having had a second account before 21 October 2026: help them choose the account to keep. Ban evasion is the exception: an extra account used while a ban or suspension was running goes to a Developer or an Owner, as above.

**Moving to a new Discord account:**

1. The player opens a ticket from the new account. They may join our Discord with it to do so (Server Rules 3.5).
2. Confirm that it is the same person only from what they tell you in the ticket and what Discord shows you. Never use the PC check or connection addresses to confirm it: the Privacy Policy does not allow that use.
3. Never during a ban or suspension.
4. Hand the ticket to a Lead GM, who checks steps 1 to 3 and passes it to a Developer. A Developer moves the characters by hand, because no staff tool does it yet.
5. The old account is closed through the account-deletion steps, and after the deletion a Lead GM types `/referral forget` with its profile number (Privacy Policy sections 5 and 6).
6. Note the move in the ticket and in staff chat, so the NEW ACCOUNT line for the new account is not read as a second account.
7. The new account's Discord age counts for voting from the day it was made. The old account's voting eligibility does not carry over.

## 4. Working tickets and GM calls (every rank)

### 4.1 Tickets

1. **Read it all first.** The player's #TAG, what happened, when, and any screenshots or logs. For a conduct case, check their record with `/discipline #TAG` (GM and above).
2. **Take it**, so nobody answers it twice. In the game, `/gm take` the call.
3. **Reply when you take it**, with one or two friendly lines: you have seen it, and what happens next. Every ticket gets an answer within 24 hours.
4. **Look before you act.** Go Invisible, teleport, watch. Ask both sides.
5. **Fix what is yours** (sections 6 to 9 say what each rank may do).
6. **Pass on what is not.** Tag the next rank up with a one-line summary, the #TAG, the time and what you already checked.
7. **Close the loop.** Tell the player what was done, close the call with a staff note, and close or hand over the ticket. Never leave a player without an answer. A ticket with no answer from the player for 24 hours may be closed.
8. **Log every character change** in the ticket: a rename (old name, new name, why), a character creation reopened, an item returned.

Who was nearby, and other players' details, go in the staff channel, never in the ticket.

### 4.2 Writing to players

- Short and friendly. Thank them for the report.
- Say what happens next, never a date you cannot keep.
- Do not argue, and do not explain internal tools. Do not paste staff chat or other players' details.
- For a crash, ask for the crash log the launcher sends with Report a Problem.
- Bugs are not tickets: send them to `/bug`, #bugs, the launcher or the website form.

### 4.3 In the game

- Take a GM call before you act (`/gm take <n>`), so two staff do not answer the same one.
- Go Invisible first if your arrival would disturb a scene. Respect scenes and step in only when you need to.
- Never use `/unstuck`, `/fixloc`, Summon or a teleport to take someone out of a jail, an arrest or a fight. The game lets staff skip those checks, so it is up to you.
- Keep quiet on voice while you are hidden.

### 4.4 Who to ask

| Question | Ask |
|---|---|
| Items, gold, skills, refunds, restitution | A Lead GM. Over 5,000 gold, or any artifact or unique item: Jake or Nate |
| Rule breaks, kicks | A GM |
| Suspensions, staff conduct, overturning a record | A Lead GM |
| Bans | A Lead GM, who brings it to a Developer or an Owner |
| Officials, citizenship, elections, jails, events | A Lead GM |
| A second Discord account | A Lead GM (section 3) |
| Race or sex changes | Jake or Nate |
| Bugs, crashes, server problems | The bug forum, with the player's #TAG and the time |
| Anything else, or anything urgent | Jake or Nate |

## 5. Discipline and punishments

### 5.1 Every punishment needs

1. **Evidence** that the reviewer can understand on its own: screenshots, video, audio, chat logs, server and game logs or other records you can check.
2. **The rule broken**, named. No identified rule, no punishment.
3. **The punishment asked for**, named by the investigator.
4. **A second, higher-ranked staff member's approval** before it is given. The investigator cannot be the only approver. This covers strikes, timeouts, suspensions, temporary and permanent bans, PK decisions and character restrictions. A warning may be given alone.
5. **A record** (5.4).

Emergency exception: for hacking, cheating, exploits, attacks on the server or deliberate disruption, act at once without a second approval, and record it afterwards.

Never punish without approval, never tamper with or hide evidence, and never raise a punishment after approval without another review.

### 5.2 The ladder

| Step | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | The player is told; nothing else happens | GM and above |
| Strike | The player is told; it counts against the account for 90 days. At 3 strikes, staff are told and a Lead GM reviews a suspension | GM and above |
| Kick | Only for a rule break in progress, with a note in staff chat | GM and above |
| Suspension | Logged out now and refused at login until it ends; up to 30 days | Lead GM and above |
| Lift a suspension, overturn a record | | Lead GM and above |
| Ban, character deletion, wiping every character of an account (`/wipechars`) | The game still lets a Lead GM run `/wipechars` today, until it is moved to Developers and Owners. A Lead GM never runs it | Developer and Owner |

Serious cases (cheating, exploits, harassment, threats, real-money trading) can skip steps (Server Rules 13.2).

### 5.3 Staff

A GM cannot discipline another staff member; a Lead GM and above can. The game stops a GM from disciplining a GM or anyone above, but not a Trial GM, so that part is up to you. Reports about a staff member go to the Owners through the Report a GM ticket.

### 5.4 Record everything

Every warning and every punishment, given in the game or on Discord, goes on the account's record. In the game: `/discipline #TAG warn <reason>` or `strike <reason>`. A warning or a strike decided on Discord is added to the same record by a Developer or Owner (the `discipline.record` setting takes warnings and strikes only). Bans are kept in the ban list, not in this record. `/discipline #TAG` shows the record.

Each record says: the player and account, the date and time, what happened, the rule broken, the evidence, the punishment asked for, who reviewed it, the decision, and the punishment given. Always give a reason the player can understand: it is shown to them.

## 6. Trial GM

Trial GM is the first staff rank. You help players, keep tickets moving and fix characters, and you hand anything bigger to a GM or Lead GM. You never decide rules, punishments or lore.

**You can:**

- read and answer every ticket in Moderation Help;
- answer GM calls in the game: list, take, go to, hand back, close and quiet them (`/gm list`, `/gm take <n>`, `/gm goto <n>`, `/gm release <n>`, `/gm close <n> [note]`, `/gm quiet`), and the GM Calls tab of the staff panel;
- use the Admin chat tab (`/admin <text>`);
- teleport to a player (`/tp <player>`) and to a named place (the panel's Teleport tab; it only ever moves you);
- go Invisible or take Ghost form from the panel's Modes tab while you look into a report;
- send a stuck player, or yourself, to the respawn point of their area (`/unstuck <player>`, `/unstuck`), with no cooldown;
- look up a character (`/charstats <player|#TAG>`) and read `/bug` reports (`/bugs [n]`);
- see every character by its name instead of "Stranger", on your own screen only (`/realnames`);
- rename a character (`/rename <player|#TAG> <new name>`), following the Server Rules on names (section 4);
- reopen character creation for the character you are playing (`/chargen`, with no name);
- play one staff character (section 2.4).

**You can't:**

- spawn or give items or gold;
- reopen character creation for other players (ask a GM);
- change skills, stats, races or character data beyond a rename;
- grant, move or remove houses, rooms, shops or faction members;
- kick, warn, strike, ban, mute or jail players, or free anyone from a jail sentence;
- edit the world, dungeons, loot or server settings.

`/help admin` shows exactly the commands you have. Every command you use is logged, marked "Trial GM".

## 7. GM

GM is the second rank. You run the day-to-day: you answer calls and tickets, fix characters, keep order in the game and preside at executions. You observe and report. Anything that creates or changes the world or the economy belongs to a Lead GM and above.

**You add, on top of a Trial GM:**

- the full staff panel (F7) to look up any player: who is online, their Discord name, skills and schools of magic;
- Summon a player to you: only to rescue them or with their consent, never into or out of a scene, a fight, an arrest or a jail;
- God and NoClip, only while investigating, building or rescuing, never in combat or near players in a scene;
- Kick (`/kick <player>`), only for a rule break in progress, with a note in staff chat;
- warnings and strikes (`/discipline #TAG warn|strike <reason>`), and reading an account's record;
- character creation for anyone (`/chargen <player|#TAG>`). A race or sex change needs Jake's or Nate's okay first, except a player's own identity reroll (a supporter tier's, or the free Skyrim-transition reroll until 30 October 2026);
- approving or refusing an execution (section 7.1);
- announcements to everyone online (`/announce <text>`);
- resetting a lost character to the starting hub (`/fixloc [player]`).

**You can't:** spawn or give items, gold, spells, shouts, skills, mastery hours or beast powers; kill a player or use Smite or Heal on Hit; place NPCs or objects or change NPC zones; ban, temp-ban or delete a character; suspend an account, lift a suspension or overturn a record; discipline another staff member; appoint or dismiss officials; grant, decline or revoke a citizenship as staff or change an election; jail players as staff or set up jail zones; use the server console; change the time or the weather or schedule restarts.

As a GM you have no staff jail tools. In play you restrain and jail like any other character: with a lawful rank (for example as a Guard), with the other player's consent, or with a rope on a downed player as any player may. Never with your staff character.

### 7.1 Executions

- An execution at an execution block, such as the one before Castle Bruma, ends a character for good, so it needs a GM (not a Trial GM) within 20 metres. Every GM in range is asked to approve or refuse.
- You cannot decide an execution you take part in, as headsman or condemned.
- Approve only when the sentence was reached in roleplay and fits the rules. If in doubt, refuse and ask a Lead GM.
- Stay at the block until the headsman confirms. With no decision in 2 minutes, or if the condemned or the headsman leaves first, there is no execution.

## 8. Lead GM

You add the tools that create or change the world and the economy. Use each one only with a reason you can write down.

**You add, on top of a GM:**

- spawning and granting items, spells, skills, beast forms and mastery hours; placing NPCs and objects; NPC zones;
- appointing and dismissing officials (`/appoint`, `/dismiss`, the F3 Court tab). The game still refuses staff in the roles of section 2.2, a Jarl who is not a Breton, Nord or Imperial, and a second leadership seat for one character;
- jail and jail zones; suspensions (`/discipline #TAG suspend <n>h|<n>d <reason>`, up to 30 days), lifting them and overturning a record; disciplining staff;
- citizenship as staff (granting, declining, revoking) and running elections (hold, restart, resume);
- striking a vote with a reason and undoing it (`/strikevote`, `/unstrikevote`, `/electionreview`): only after a review, and within the Privacy Policy (section 3);
- deciding referrals held for review, taking a referral back, and erasing a deleted account's referral record (`/referral forget`);
- `/faction leader`, `/faction remove`, `/faction override` (lifts a membership limit once), faction halls;
- curses and beast forms (`/beastform`, the Powers tab): only for an approved event or to put right a bug;
- Thalmor dossiers (add and strike), skill boosts, smithing manuals, a faith reset (`/deity reset`);
- the time, the weather, warbands, raids and war (scheduling a restart is for Developers and Owners only);
- restraining anyone at once, without a lawful rank.

**Items and gold:**

- Do not spawn items for players where the game has no way to earn them; ask for a bug report instead (Jake, 4 October).
- Restitution needs evidence first: who lost what, when, where, and who had it.
- Over 5,000 gold, or any artifact or unique item: Jake or Nate decides.

You do not ban, delete a character from the staff panel or wipe an account: bring those to a Developer or an Owner. The game still lets a Lead GM run `/wipechars` (it destroys every character of an account) until it is moved to Developers and Owners: never run it.

## 9. Developers and Owners

**Developers** add bans (in the game and by account), deleting a character from the staff panel, wiping every character of an account (`/wipechars`) and scheduling restarts (`/schedule`), and fix the game. A Developer also moves a player's characters to a new Discord account by hand (section 3).

**Owners** can do everything. Only the Owners may seat a Jarl who is not a Breton, Nord or Imperial, and read Report a GM tickets. Final decisions on rules, punishments and lore are Jake's or Nate's, who have equal authority.
